## 1. Core Principles of Graha-Bhava Dynamics

To understand how a planet affects you in a specific house:

1. **Benefics vs. Malefics**:
   - **Natural Benefics (*Shubha Grahas*)**: Guru (Jupiter), Shukra (Venus), waxing Chandra (Moon), well-associated Budha (Mercury). They protect and expand the affairs of whichever house they occupy or aspect.
   - **Natural Malefics (*Krura/Papa Grahas*)**: Shani (Saturn), Mangal (Mars), Surya (Sun), Rahu, Ketu. They bring discipline, friction, separation, or heat to the house, but excel in **Upachaya houses (3, 6, 10, 11)** where grit leads to victory.
2. **House Categories**:
   - **Kendras (1, 4, 7, 10)**: Vishnu Sthanas. Planets here become prominently visible and drive the major actions of your life.
   - **Trikonas (1, 5, 9)**: Lakshmi Sthanas. Bring good fortune, blessings from past lives, and ease.
   - **Dusthanas (6, 8, 12)**: Houses of obstacles, transformation, and expenditures. Benefics here lose some worldly strength; malefics here can fight off enemies or trigger deep spiritual evolution.
   - **Upachayas (3, 6, 10, 11)**: Growth houses. Planets here improve their results as you mature.

## 4. Birth Chart (*Janma Kundli*) vs. Live Transit (*Gochara*)

In Vedic Astrology (*Jyotish*), **Janma Kundli** and **Gochara** are the two foundational gears of human destiny. 

A famous classical analogy captures their dynamic:

> **"Janma Kundli is the seed planted in the soil.**  
> **Dasha is the season.**  
> **Gochara is the daily weather that triggers the sprout to bloom."**

---

### The 3-Tier Vedic Timing Engine

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        THE 3-TIER VEDIC ENGINE                         │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  1. THE PROMISE (Janma Kundli)  ──>  "Can this event happen in life?"   │
│  2. THE SEASON  (Vimshottari Dasha) ──>  "Is the timing ripe?"         │
│  3. THE TRIGGER (Gochara Transits)  ──>  "WHEN does it manifest today?"│
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Golden Rule of Jyotish:**  
> *Gochara (Transits) cannot grant what the Janma Kundli (Birth Chart) does not promise.*  
> Even if an exalted Jupiter transits your 11th house of wealth today, you will not win a huge lottery unless your birth chart has a specific, powerful *Dhana Yoga* (wealth promise) active in your natal blueprint.

---

### A. Janma Kundli — The Birth Blueprint (Static)

* **What it is:** A frozen photograph of the cosmos taken at the exact second, minute, and geo-coordinates (latitude/longitude) of your birth.
* **Permanence:** It **never changes** throughout your entire life.
* **What it Reveals:**
  * **Prarabdha Karma:** The specific portion of past-life karma chosen to bear fruit in this current physical incarnation.
  * **Core Hardware:** Your physical constitution, appearance, psychological wiring, intellectual capacity, and natural vulnerabilities.
  * **Yogas & Combinations:** Permanent alignments such as *Raja Yogas* (fame/leadership), *Dhana Yogas* (wealth), or challenges like *Kuja Dosha* or *Kemadruma Yoga*.
  * **The "Ceiling" and "Floor":** The maximum heights achievable and inherent life lessons to master.

---

### B. Gochara — The Live Transit Clock (Dynamic)

* **What it is:** The actual, ongoing movement of the planets through the zodiac in the sky **right now** (this is what our Jyotishi app's ephemeris calculates live).
* **Speed of Transits Across the Rashis:**
  * 🌙 **Moon (Chandra):** Changes Rashi every **2.25 days** (shapes daily moods and emotional reactions).
  * ☀️ **Sun, Mercury, Venus:** Change Rashi every **~30 days** (shapes monthly themes, focus, and energy).
  * ♂ **Mars (Mangal):** Changes Rashi every **~45 days** (shapes drives, conflicts, physical stamina).
  * ♃ **Jupiter (Guru):** Changes Rashi every **~1 year** (brings yearly windows of expansion, learning, and grace).
  * ☊/☋ **Rahu & Ketu:** Stay in a Rashi for **~1.5 years** (shifts karmic obsession and spiritual release).
  * ♄ **Saturn (Shani):** Stays in a Rashi for **~2.5 to 3 years** (the master taskmaster restructuring duty and endurance).

#### Why is Gochara Counted from the Moon Sign (*Chandra Rashi*)?
In classical Vedic astrology, transits are evaluated primarily from your **Moon Sign** rather than your rising sign (*Lagna*):
* The Moon represents **Manas** (the perceptual mind, emotional nervous system, and experiential awareness).
* You experience the world through your mind. When a transit planet enters a house relative to your natal Moon, it immediately shifts your psychological climate and experiential reality.

### D. Practical Real-World Scenarios

#### Scenario 1: Career Promotion
* **Janma Kundli:** Strong 10th house with Sun and Mars, promising executive leadership.
* **Dasha:** You enter an auspicious Sun or Mars planetary period (*Mahadasha*).
* **Gochara:** Today, live Jupiter transits into your 10th or 11th house from the Moon, aspecting natal planets.  
  * **Result:** **Promotion approved today.** (The birth chart promised it, the Dasha sanctioned it, and the transit delivered it).

#### Scenario 2: Sade Sati (Saturn's 7.5-Year Transit)
* **What it is:** A pure **Gochara phenomenon**. It occurs when live Saturn transits the 12th house, 1st house, and 2nd house relative to your natal Moon.
* **Why it matters:** 
  * If your **Janma Kundli** has a well-placed, dignified Saturn (e.g., in Libra or Capricorn), Sade Sati builds massive resilience, clears old debts, and crowns you with lasting worldly achievements.
  * If your **Janma Kundli** has a compromised Saturn, the same transit demands deep humility, breaks ego illusions, and tests endurance.
