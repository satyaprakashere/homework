## 💡 Quick Tips

### 1. S-Expressions
Everything is a list: `(operator operand1 operand2)`.

### 2. Functional
Strong emphasis on recursion and first-class functions.

### 3. Macros
Powerful hygienic macros with `syntax-rules`.
